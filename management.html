    Using the app¶

    This page explains the AI Firewall app: what each screen shows and what you can do on it.

    AI Firewall protects you in the background. You do not need to open the app for it to work. Open it when you want to see what it did, or when something looks wrong.

    The app at a glance¶

    The app has a menu on the left. It has three groups.

    Group Screen What it is for
    Monitor Dashboard Today's summary: is protection running, and what did it do?
    Monitor Activity Every request the firewall saw, and what it did with each one.
    Configure Protection What the firewall looks for, and what happens when it finds something.
    Configure AI tools protection The skills and MCP servers installed on this computer.
    Configure Detection engine The on-device model that finds sensitive data.
    This device Device & coverage Connection to your company, and which AI traffic is covered.
    This device Updates The installed version and how updates arrive.
    This device Help Troubleshooting, common questions and who to ask.

    At the bottom of the menu, a status card shows if protection is on, for example "Protection is on". It also shows who sets the rules ("Managed by IT" or "Local settings") and the mode. Below it you see the app version and a button to switch between light and dark theme. The button at the top of the menu folds it to icons.

    There is no on/off switch in the app. Your company decides when protection is on. You can close the app from the tray menu (Quit), but then protection stops. See When AI Firewall is not running.

    The i button at the top of every screen ("Why this status?") shows the checks behind the current status. For the meaning of each status, see Check status.

    Who is in charge: you or your IT team¶

    Your IT team sets the rules. You cannot change the mode or the list of data types in the app. You can see them, so you know what protects you. Where a rule is set by your IT team, the screen says "Set by your IT team. To change it, contact them."

    Things you can change on your own computer:

    • Company patterns (Protection screen).
    • Detection model and the languages for reading text in images (Detection engine screen).
    • Notifications, Start with this laptop, and saving, restoring or resetting your settings (Device & coverage screen).
    • Command-line tools: which tools go through the firewall (Device & coverage screen).

    Restoring or resetting your settings never changes the rules set by your IT team.

    Dashboard¶

    Shows today's picture:

    • A headline, for example "No AI traffic was inspected on this laptop today."
    • A bar showing how requests were handled, and tiles such as most common find, busiest AI tool and number of AI tools inspected.
    • Traffic by AI tool: which AI tools were used today.
    • What was protected: what kinds of data were found. "Adjust" opens the Protection screen.

    Two buttons take you on: Review today's activity and See what is protected.

    If the lists are empty, nothing has been scanned yet. Requests to AI services will appear as you use them.

    Activity¶

    Everything stays on your computer. There are three tabs.

    • Apps: which apps talked to which AI services. Open a row to see details.
    • Requests: every request, newest first, with time, size and scan time. Open a row to see what was found and what was done.
    • Conversations: the same traffic shown as chats. You can search messages, tools and chats, and filter by who sent the message or by tool.

    If the list is empty, the screen tells you why. For example, protection may be paused, or AI traffic is not reaching the firewall yet. In that case open Device & coverage to see which part is missing.

    Protection¶

    This screen shows what is protecting you. The mode and the data types are read-only. You can look, but not change them.

    When sensitive data is found in a request. Three modes:

    Name What happens
    Record only The request is sent as typed. You get a log entry so you know what left your computer.
    Replace Details are swapped for placeholders, then put back in the answer you read.
    Block The request is not sent. You see what was found and can edit the prompt and try again.

    Details of what each mode does, including the exceptions for machine secrets, are on the page What is protected.

    What the firewall looks for. 32 types of data in four groups: People, Credentials, Financial and Government identity. The heading shows how many are on, for example "10 of 32 types active". Each type shows how many were found today. A type marked "Not detected yet" cannot be found by the firewall at the moment. Credential detection is always on.

    A type that is switched off is not replaced, but the firewall still records that it saw it, so your IT team can spot a gap.

    AI services this applies to. The AI services that are inspected. A service that is not on the list is not inspected.

    Things only your company would recognise. Patterns specific to your company, for example an internal project code. You can add your own with Add a company pattern, and remove them again. They stay on your computer. Patterns pushed by your IT team are separate and read-only.

    Never replace these. A list of terms that are never replaced. It is set by your IT team and you cannot change it. If something is replaced that should not be, tell your IT team.

    AI tools protection¶

    Lists the skills and MCP servers found in the configuration files on your computer. Nothing is sent while it looks.

    • A skill is a set of instructions the AI model follows. An MCP server is a program the AI tool can call.
    • Scan now looks again.
    • For each item you see its name, type (MCP server or skill), the tool it belongs to, where it is configured, and a risk level (high, medium, low) with the reason.
    • "Could not be read" means a configuration file could not be parsed.

    This is a list, not an approval. Nobody has reviewed these items, and an MCP server that opens its own connection is not inspected by the firewall. [TBC: confirm wording with the product team.]

    Detection engine¶

    Shows how the firewall finds data. Two things run on every request: pattern matching (always on) and a language model that finds names, addresses and similar free text.

    • Model in use: the models you can choose from, with size, languages and download state. You can choose a model on your own computer.
    • Text inside images: the languages used to read text in pictures. You can add or remove a language.

    These choices are local to your computer. Your IT team does not lock them.

    Device & coverage¶

    Shows the state of this computer.

    This device: status, your company's server, enrolment state, when the app last checked in, device ID, policy version, app version, and whether the certificate is trusted ("Yes — installed"). If the connection to your company's server fails, the screen explains why and offers Try connecting again. Protection keeps running either way. The connection only affects settings and reports.

    Coverage: which AI traffic passes through the firewall.

    • Browsers and desktop apps
    • Command-line tools (Claude Code, Codex and Kimi Code)
    • Tools that those tools start

    Each one is shown as Covered, Partial or Not covered, with a reason.

    AI services covered: the services and addresses that are inspected.

    Install certificate. If the certificate is missing, a banner appears at the top: AI Firewall needs to install a security certificate to inspect AI traffic. Select Install certificate and approve the prompt.

    Connect a command-line tool. Claude Code, Codex and Kimi Code do not follow the system proxy, so they are off until you turn them on. Select this button, choose the tool, and follow the three steps. AI Firewall then changes that tool's own settings so its requests go through the firewall. Turning protection off, or uninstalling, puts the settings back. Start a new session of the tool afterwards. Your IT team can also turn tools on for the whole company.

    What you are told about. Choose which notifications you get. Some are always on and cannot be turned off, for example "A request was not sent" and "Protection stopped".

    This laptop. Start with this laptop, save a copy of your settings, restore from a copy, or reset to defaults.

    Updates¶

    Shows the installed version, the newest version and when the app last checked.

    • Check now looks for an update.
    • Restart now appears when an update is ready. The restart takes a few seconds, and AI traffic pauses briefly.
    • Every update is checked before it is installed: it must be signed by ScienceSoft and newer than the current version. The app never asks you for a password.
    • If your IT team manages updates, the screen says so. You stay protected on the installed version until they deliver the update.
    • If the app cannot update itself on your computer, an orange banner says so and asks you to contact your IT administrator.

    Help and Contact us¶

    Help has troubleshooting steps and common questions, including:

    • Protection is on but nothing appears in Activity
    • Names are not replaced, but emails and card numbers are
    • Something was replaced that should not have been
    • My command-line tool is not being inspected
    • A setting will not change
    • What happens if the firewall stops working?

    It also has Copy details, to send technical details to support, and Contact us.

    Contact us: choose Ask a question, Report a problem or Request a call, and write a message. The message is checked on your computer first. Personal data is replaced before it is sent, and you see exactly what will be sent before you press Send this. It needs a connection to your company's workspace.

    Remove this app is on the Help screen on macOS. On Windows, remove it in Settings > Apps. See Uninstall.

    Messages you may see¶

    For the protection status, see Check status. These are other messages.

    Message What it means
    Reduced detection on this laptop Protection is on, but part of detection is not running (for example name detection). Restart the computer or install a pending update. If it stays, contact your IT team.
    Audit log upload Reports to your company's server are failing. Protection keeps working, and records wait on your computer until the problem is fixed.
    Connections to AI services The firewall could not connect to an AI service. The message says what failed and who can fix it.
    Downloading protection… The detection model is downloading.
    Update needs attention The last update did not finish, or the app cannot update itself on this computer. Select Retry now or Check now, or contact your IT administrator.

    Trial only¶

    In a trial build, the app asks for a server address and an enrollment code from your welcome email. It shows a notice when the trial is about to end, and says that when it ends the device stops reporting to the workspace, while protection on the computer continues. See the [Trial] User and Role Guide.
