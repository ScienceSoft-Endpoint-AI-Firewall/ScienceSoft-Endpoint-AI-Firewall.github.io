    Install¶

    This page explains how ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall gets onto your computer and how to check that it works.

    What you need¶

    • A Windows or macOS computer. There is no Linux version.
    • Permission to approve a security certificate on this computer. On Windows this needs an administrator account. On macOS you need your login password.
    • An internet connection.

    Two ways to install¶

    Your company chooses one of these.

    Way 1: You install it Way 2: Your IT team installs it
    Who does the work You Your IT team, on many computers at once
    What you receive An installer file from your IT team (.exe on Windows, .dmg on macOS) Nothing. The app just appears.
    What you do Follow the steps below Approve the certificate if asked, then check that it works (Steps 3 and 4)

    Way 1: Install it yourself¶

    Step 1. Run the installer¶

    1. Open the installer file you received.
    2. Follow the on-screen steps and finish the installation.
    3. Open AI Firewall.

    Step 2. Connect to your company¶

    The enrollment code is the key that joins this computer to your company's workspace. You do not type it in the app. It is given to the installer as an argument when the installer is run. Your IT team does this: they either run the installer for you, or give you the command to run. The switches are described in Roll out the agent.

    Treat the enrollment code like a password. Do not paste it into chats, tickets or AI tools.

    Trial only

    In a trial, the app asks for a server address and an enrollment code from your welcome email. Your ScienceSoft representative can send you the trial guide.

    Step 3. Approve the certificate¶

    AI traffic is encrypted. To read a request before it leaves your computer, ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall needs your computer to trust it. It does this with a certificate.

    1. A prompt appears. On Windows it is a security prompt. On macOS it asks for your login password.
    2. Read it. The certificate is issued to AI Firewall Local CA.
    3. Approve it.

    About this certificate:

    • It is made on your computer and works on this computer only.
    • Its private key never leaves your computer.
    • If you decline, ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall cannot read encrypted traffic. It will tell you this. It will not pretend that you are protected.

    Step 4. Check that it works¶

    1. Open Device & coverage in the app.
    2. Find Certificate trust. It must say Yes — installed.

    If it says anything else, see If something goes wrong below.

    Way 2: Your IT team installs it¶

    Your IT team installs ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall for the whole company. You do not need to download or set up anything.

    You may still see the certificate prompt described in Step 3. Approve it. Then check that it works with Step 4.

    Your IT team can find the deployment details in Roll out the agent.

    What your company can see¶

    ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall works on your computer. The text you type, your files and your images are checked on your computer. They are not sent anywhere to be scanned.

    If your computer is connected to a workspace, the workspace's administrators can see what was detected (how many values and which types) and what was done about it. By default the message is also recorded there, with the private values already replaced. So they can read what you wrote, but not the personal data found in it. An administrator can choose to keep less (the setting is called Data Capture Mode).

    The private values themselves are not collected. A computer that is not connected to a workspace sends nothing.

    If something goes wrong¶

    What you see What to do
    Certificate trust is not Yes — installed You may have declined the prompt. A banner at the top of the app offers Install certificate. Select it, follow the short steps and approve the system prompt this time.
    The app says the code was not accepted Check that you copied the whole code. If it still fails, ask your IT team for a new one.
    The app says it cannot reach the server Check your internet connection. A company network or VPN may block the request. Ask your IT team.

    If ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall is closed or stopped, AI services are either unavailable or reached without protection, depending on how it was installed. See When ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall is not running. Start the app again.

    For more problems, see Troubleshooting.
