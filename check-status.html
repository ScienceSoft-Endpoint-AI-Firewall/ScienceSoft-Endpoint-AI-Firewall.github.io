    Check status¶

    This page explains how to check if ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall is protecting you, what each status means, and what to do about it.

    Where to see the status¶

    • The status card at the bottom of the menu in the app. It shows the status, who sets the rules ("Managed by IT" or "Local settings") and the mode.
    • The Dashboard. The first line shows the same status, in capital letters.
    • The i button at the top right of every screen ("Why this status?"). It lists the checks behind the status and shows which ones passed.

    The i window shows three checks:

    Check What it means
    Traffic is steered here Your operating system sends AI-tool traffic to the firewall, so it can be read.
    Detection engine is at full strength The on-device model that finds sensitive data is running normally.
    Interception confirmed A test request went through the firewall in the last few minutes.

    When all three pass, the window says "Running and protecting: every check passed." If a check fails, it is marked and the status above changes.

    Example: the status card when your organisation has paused protection.

    What each status means¶

    The first rows are the good ones. The later rows need attention.

    Status What it means What to do
    Protecting this device (or Protection is on, when your IT team manages the computer) AI traffic on this computer is checked before it leaves. Nothing.
    Protected — browsers and proxy-aware apps Browsers and apps that follow the system proxy setting are protected. Other programs are not routed through the firewall at the moment. The screen gives a reason. Nothing you can press. If it stays, tell your IT team.
    Inspecting with limits Traffic is checked, but one part of detection is not available on this computer (for example the name model, or reading text in images). The screen says which part and what it means. Restart the computer or install a pending update. If it stays, contact your IT team.
    Mode not confirmed Traffic is checked, but the app could not confirm that the engine is using the mode that was chosen (Record only, Replace or Block). Treat the mode as not applied. See If you see "Mode not confirmed" below. Wait a minute, then restart the app. If it stays, tell your IT team.
    Checking protection The app is checking. This is normal for a few seconds after start-up. Wait. If it stays for more than a few minutes, the app may be out of date and unable to run the check. It should update itself. If not, contact your IT team.
    Turning on protection / Turning off protection A change is in progress. Wait.
    Not inspecting traffic The protection is running, but AI traffic is not reaching it. Nothing is being checked. Open Device & coverage and look at Certificate trust and System proxy to see which part is missing. Tell your IT team.
    Protection could not start The app could not start protection. The message gives the cause and the fix. Follow the message. If it fails again, contact your IT team.
    Protection has stopped Protection stopped working. The app is trying to restart it. See When protection has stopped. Wait a moment. If it does not come back, contact your IT team.
    Protection is paused by your organisation Your IT team paused protection. Only they can turn it back on. See When protection is paused. Contact your IT team if you did not expect this.
    Some settings remain Protection is off, but some settings could not be removed. AI tools on this computer may not connect. Restart the app. If it stays, contact your IT team.
    Another ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall app is protecting this computer A second ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall app on this computer checks traffic from desktop apps. Browsers may still be protected by this app. Uninstall one of the two apps, or reinstall this one.
    Not set up yet This computer is not enrolled. Your IT team needs to finish the setup. Contact your IT team.

    If you see "Mode not confirmed"¶

    Your AI traffic is still being checked. What is not certain is which mode is in use. Until the status goes back to normal, treat the chosen mode (for example Replace) as not applied: private data might not be replaced or blocked.

    The app asks the engine to confirm the mode, and tries three times. The status appears when the engine does not answer in time. Common reasons:

    • The engine restarted, and has not confirmed the mode again yet.
    • The engine was not ready when the mode was set (for example just after start-up or a change of rules).
    • A setting pushed by your IT team did not reach the engine.

    What to do:

    1. Wait a minute. When the app sees that the engine is answering again, it sends the mode again by itself.
    2. Restart the app.
    3. If the status stays, tell your IT team. Copy the details from Help (see How to report a problem).

    When protection has stopped¶

    What happens to your AI requests depends on how ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall was installed.

    • Windows with the system guard, and macOS installed from the .pkg: AI services are blocked on this computer until protection restarts. Nothing is sent without being checked.
    • Any other installation: AI requests still go out, and personal data in them is not checked or replaced. Do not send private data to AI tools until the status is back to "Protecting this device".

    In both cases the app is trying to restart the protection by itself.

    When protection is paused¶

    A pause always comes from your organisation. There is no button for you to turn protection back on.

    The title is the same in both cases: Protection is paused by your organisation. The sentence under it tells them apart:

    • "Traffic is still routed through the app, but nothing is being inspected." Nothing is checked, but the connection is not broken.
    • "AI traffic from this device is going out without being inspected." Nothing is routed through the app. AI traffic goes out without being checked.

    In both cases, do not send private data to AI tools until your IT team turns protection back on.

    How to report a problem¶

    1. Open Help and select Copy details.
    2. Select Contact us, describe the problem and paste the details. Personal data in the message is replaced before it is sent, and you see what will be sent.
    3. Or send the details to your IT team.
