    Troubleshooting¶

    This page helps you when AI Firewall does not behave as you expect. Find your problem in the list, then follow the steps. If a status in the app is not green, read Check status first.

    Problem 1. Protection is on, but Activity is empty¶

    Activity only fills after you send something to an AI service from this computer.

    1. Use an AI tool (for example, send a short message in a browser).
    2. Open Activity again.
    3. If it is still empty, open Device & coverage. Look at Browsers and desktop apps.
    4. If it is not Covered, the certificate or the system proxy is not in place. Your AI traffic is going out directly, without being checked. Look at Certificate trust and System proxy on the same screen to see which part is missing. Then see Problem 6, or contact your IT team.

    The empty Activity screen also gives a reason, for example "Protection is paused" or "Nothing is reaching the firewall yet".

    If you only use Claude Code, Codex or Kimi Code, see Problem 4: they are off until they are turned on.

    Problem 2. Names are not replaced, or nothing is replaced¶

    There are two different causes. Check the first one first.

    1. The mode. Open Protection. If the mode is Record only, the request is sent as you typed it. Only machine secrets, such as API keys, and your organisation's keywords are replaced. This is the default for new teams. Ask your IT team if this is what they want.
    2. The detection model. Emails and card numbers are found by patterns, which always run. Names are found by a language model. If it is still downloading, or could not start, names are not replaced. Open Detection engine to see which model is in use and whether it started. A notice "Downloading protection…" means the download is still going.

    Arabic names are not detected yet.

    Problem 3. Something was replaced that should not have been¶

    The detection model does not know which names or words are safe in your work.

    Tell your IT team the exact word and what it was replaced with. They can add an exception for your team. You cannot add exceptions yourself: the list of words that are never replaced is set by your IT team.

    Problem 4. My command-line tool is not checked¶

    Command-line tools do not follow the system proxy setting. They are off until someone turns them on. Until then, their requests are not checked.

    1. Open Device & coverage. Look at Command-line tools. It must say Covered.
    2. If it does not, select Connect a command-line tool, choose Claude Code, Codex or Kimi Code, and follow the steps. Your IT team can also turn tools on for the whole company.
    3. Start a new session of the tool. A session that was already running keeps its old settings.
    4. Claude Code and Kimi Code cannot both be covered at once.
    5. Any other command-line tool is not covered. See What is protected.

    Problem 5. A setting will not change¶

    If your IT team manages your computer, they set the rules. The mode and the list of data types are always read-only in the app. A banner says "Set by your IT team. To change it, contact them." on the screens where your IT team sets a rule. To change a rule, contact them.

    You can change company patterns, the detection model, image languages, notifications and the start-up setting yourself.

    Problem 6. Certificate trust is not "Yes — installed"¶

    Without the certificate, AI Firewall cannot read encrypted AI traffic.

    1. A banner at the top of the app says AI Firewall needs to install a security certificate. Select Install certificate.
    2. Approve the prompt from your operating system. On Windows it needs an administrator account. On macOS it asks for your login password.
    3. Open Device & coverage. Certificate trust must now say Yes — installed.

    If you declined the prompt, repeat the steps and approve it this time. More details are on Install.

    Problem 7. The app cannot reach your company's server¶

    Protection on your computer keeps running. The connection only affects settings and reports.

    Open Device & coverage. The line Why explains what happened.

    What the screen says What it means and what to do
    The server rejected this device's credentials, or does not recognise this device any more The enrolment was probably reset or removed. Contact your IT team: the device needs to be enrolled again.
    This device can't reach the server Check your network, VPN or firewall. This usually clears on its own.
    The server is returning errors Usually temporary. Wait, then try again.

    Select Try connecting again to ask the server to accept this device again.

    Problem 8. Teams or Outlook cannot sign in¶

    To cover Microsoft 365 Copilot, AI Firewall also checks graph.microsoft.com. The Teams, Outlook and Office desktop apps pin their certificate for that address, so they may fail to sign in or send messages while protection is on. Tell your IT team. Turning protection off restores them, but only your IT team can do that.

    Problem 9. AI tools stopped working when I closed the app¶

    On Windows with the system guard, and on macOS installed from the .pkg, the AI services that AI Firewall covers are blocked while the app is not running. This protects you: requests are not sent unchecked. Start the app again. Your IT team can grant an exception.

    On macOS installed from the .dmg, nothing is blocked. If the app is closed, AI requests go out without being checked.

    Problem 10. AI tools feel slower¶

    A little delay is normal. Dashboard shows the median scan time measured on your own computer. Large screenshots take longer, because the text inside them is read first. If the delay is large, contact your IT team.

    Problem 11. A window asks me to confirm¶

    Your organisation can ask you to confirm before you use a service or upload a file. Choose Continue to go ahead, or Cancel. If you do not answer in time, the message "The request was cancelled" appears. Send the request again to be asked again.

    Still stuck?¶

    1. Open Help and select Copy details.
    2. Select Contact us, choose Report a problem, and write what happened. Paste the details.
    3. Your message is checked on your computer first. Personal data is replaced before it is sent, and you see what will be sent.
    4. Or send the details to your IT team.
