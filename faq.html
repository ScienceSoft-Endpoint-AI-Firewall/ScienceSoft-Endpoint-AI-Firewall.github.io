    FAQ¶

    Short answers to the questions people ask most. For steps to fix a problem, see Troubleshooting.

    Privacy¶

    Can my manager read my chats?¶

    This tool has two jobs: it keeps your company's confidential data out of the prompts that your apps send to AI services, and it collects as little as possible about you while doing that.

    Plainly:

    • The workspace's administrators can see what was detected: how many values were found, and which types. They cannot see the values themselves.
    • If your computer is connected to a workspace, the message is also recorded there, by default, with those values replaced. So they can read what you wrote, but not the personal data that was found in it. An administrator can choose to keep less (a short excerpt, or no message text at all). The setting is called Data Capture Mode.
    • What the AI service receives depends on your company's setting. In Replace mode, values that the firewall is sure enough about are replaced before sending. In Record only mode, your message is sent as typed, apart from machine secrets such as API keys.
    • A computer that is not connected to a workspace sends nothing.

    Is my data sent anywhere to be scanned?¶

    No. Your messages, files and images are checked on your computer. The detection model runs on your computer too.

    Does AI Firewall read all my internet traffic?¶

    No. Only traffic to AI services is sent through it. Every other site goes straight out, untouched and unread.

    Does ScienceSoft see my data?¶

    Only if your computer is connected to a server run by ScienceSoft, as in a trial. Then ScienceSoft can see what that workspace records. ScienceSoft can disconnect a computer from its server on request.

    In a normal installation, the workspace belongs to your company, and ScienceSoft does not see it.

    Why does it install a certificate? Is that safe?¶

    AI traffic is encrypted. To check a request before it leaves, your computer has to trust AI Firewall. The certificate does that.

    • It is made on your computer.
    • It works on this computer only.
    • Its private key never leaves your computer.

    Day to day¶

    Do I need to do anything every day?¶

    No. AI Firewall works in the background. Open the app when you want to see what it did, or when something looks wrong.

    Can I turn protection off?¶

    No. There is no on/off switch in the app. Your company decides when protection is on.

    You can close the app from the tray menu, but then nothing is checked. Depending on how AI Firewall was installed, AI services are then either blocked or reached without being checked (see What is protected). Your IT team can see that the device has stopped reporting.

    Does it slow down my AI tools?¶

    A little. The Dashboard shows the median scan time measured on your own computer. Large screenshots take longer, because the text inside them is read first.

    Why was a name replaced when it was my own?¶

    The detection model does not know which names are safe in your work. Tell your IT team, and they can add an exception for your team. You cannot add one yourself.

    What happens if AI Firewall stops working?¶

    It says so, and does not pretend otherwise. The status changes (see Check status), and Device & coverage stops showing the affected traffic as covered.

    What happens to your AI requests depends on how AI Firewall was installed:

    • On Windows with the system guard, and on macOS installed from the .pkg, AI services are blocked until protection is back.
    • In other installations, requests go out without being checked. Do not send private data until the status is back to "Protecting this device".

    What is protected¶

    Does it work with Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini?¶

    Yes, in your browser and in desktop apps built on web technology (for example Claude Desktop). Claude Code, Codex and Kimi Code are covered too, after they are turned on (see What is protected). The full list, and what is not covered, is on the same page.

    What does it do with files and images?¶

    Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF and OpenDocument files are opened on your computer and their text is checked. For images and screenshots, the text inside the picture is read on your computer. In Replace mode, a cleaned copy of a file is sent instead of the original.

    A file that cannot be read (for example an encrypted file) is recorded as not checked. By default it is still sent. Your IT team can choose to refuse such files instead.

    Which languages does it support?¶

    The detection model, the ScienceSoft PII Encoder, supports 7 languages: English, Russian, Arabic, Spanish, German, Italian and French. Arabic person names are not detected yet (see What is protected).

    What is not protected?¶

    An AI tool that is not named on What is protected is not protected. Examples: other command-line tools, scripts, and the JetBrains AI Assistant.

    Updates¶

    Does the app update itself?¶

    Yes, or your IT team delivers updates, if they manage them. Every update must be signed by ScienceSoft and newer than the version you have, and the app never asks you for a password. When an update is ready, select Restart now. The restart takes a few seconds, and AI traffic pauses briefly.
