    User Guide¶

    This guide is for people who use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini on a computer where ScienceSoft AI Endpoint Firewall (called AI Firewall in this guide) is installed and managed by their company. If you are trying AI Firewall in a trial, ask your ScienceSoft representative for the trial guide instead.

    What AI Firewall does¶

    When you send a message, a file or a picture to an AI tool, it can contain private data. This can be a name, a card number, a password or a company secret.

    AI Firewall checks your request on your computer, before it leaves. If it finds private data, it follows the rule your company has set. It can record it, replace it with a placeholder, or stop the request.

    What stays private, and what is recorded¶

    Both are true, so we say both.

    • Stays on your computer: the checking itself. Your messages, files and images are not sent anywhere to be scanned. The private values that were found are not collected.
    • Recorded: if your computer is connected to a workspace, the workspace's administrators can see what was detected: how many values were found and which types. By default the message is also recorded there, with the private values replaced. So they can read what you wrote, but not the personal data found in it. How much is recorded is set by an administrator (the setting is called Data Capture Mode).
    • Whose workspace: normally the workspace belongs to your company. If your computer is connected to a server run by ScienceSoft, as in a trial, ScienceSoft can see what that workspace records.
    • Not connected to a workspace: nothing is sent.

    Good to know first¶

    • You do not need to do anything every day. AI Firewall works in the background.
    • Your company sets the rules. Many settings are locked. If a rule gets in your way, ask your IT team.
    • It protects only what it covers. An AI tool that is not named on the coverage page is not protected.
    • It must be running. If the app is stopped, protection may stop too, depending on how it was installed. See the coverage page.

    Need help?¶

    Open Help in the app. It has troubleshooting steps and a Contact us form. You can also ask your IT team.

    Using the Admin Portal, or deploying AI Firewall to many computers? See the Admin Guide.
